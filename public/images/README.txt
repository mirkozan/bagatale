ORIGINAL IMAGE ASSETS
=====================

Place the final logo and other original images in this folder. Files in
public/ are copied into the production build and are available on Netlify.

Expected logo filename: logo.svg
Other suggested filenames: home-hero.jpg, about-portrait.jpg,
video-01.jpg, video-02.jpg, video-03.jpg

Recommended preparation: use SVG for logos, JPG/WebP for photographs, and
1920 x 1080 px for 16:9 video thumbnails.
